# Math-Physics Roadmap Structure

## Vision
Refresh rusty math/physics background to enable:
- Mathematics of Quantitative Finance
- Quantum Computing (down the line)

## Subtopics

### 1. Foundations (current)
Quick refresh of core prerequisites:

- **Calculus** — Limits, derivatives, integrals, sequences & series, multivariable calculus, differential equations
- **Statistics/Probability** — Distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, regression, time series, stochastic processes
- **Quantum Mechanics** — Mathematical formalism, measurement, angular momentum, approximation methods
- **Theoretical CS** — Automata, computability, complexity, intro to quantum computing

### 2. Quantitative Finance (future)
- Asset pricing
- Risk management
- Stochastic calculus
- Derivatives

### 3. Quantum Computing (future)
- Quantum algorithms
- Quantum information theory
- Error correction

---

## Phase Naming Convention

For each subtopic, phases follow a progression:
- `foundations` → `intermediate` → `advanced` (or domain-specific names)

Within each phase, goals are ordered to build mastery progressively.
